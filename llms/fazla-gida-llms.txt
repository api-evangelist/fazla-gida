# Fazla (Fazla Gida / Whole Surplus)

> Turkish technology-driven waste management and circular-economy company (founded 2017, Istanbul). A digital decision-support platform that helps B2B clients across the supply chain prevent waste at the source and recover value from surplus inventory through secondary-sales marketplace, donation, animal-feed conversion, and recycling modules. First B Corp-certified waste-management organization in Turkey; backed by 500 Global.
